# 1. Get an IDA account and ADNI access

*Last verified: 28 October 2026 (IDA User Manual, June 2026 version; ADNI data access page).*

ADNI data are hosted by LONI on the **Image & Data Archive (IDA)**. You need two things: an IDA account, which anyone
can create, and **approval for the ADNI project**, which is reviewed by a committee.

## Step 1: create an IDA account

1. Go to <https://ida.loni.usc.edu/> → **Log In** (top right) → **Create Account**.
2. Enter your email. A security code arrives from `ida@loni.usc.edu`; check your spam folder.
3. Enter the code, fill in the account form and click **Complete**.

Use your **institutional email**. The ADNI review checks your affiliation.

## Step 2: apply for ADNI

1. Open the ADNI application form: <https://ida.loni.usc.edu/collaboration/access/appApply.jsp?project=ADNI>
   (also linked from <https://adni.loni.usc.edu/data-samples/adni-data/>).
2. Fill it in. ADNI says the application "must include the investigator's institutional affiliation and the proposed
   uses of the ADNI" data. "Incomplete applications or those without a clear focus will not receive approval."
3. Accept the Data Use Agreement ([read the rules first](00-rules.md)).

Tips for the "proposed use" text:

- Be concrete: the question, the data you need (for example "T1 MRI and diagnosis at all visits"), and the method
  (for example "train and validate a 3D CNN to predict conversion from MCI to dementia").
- If students or colleagues will use the data, add them as co-investigators. Each person who uses the data must be
  on the application.

## Step 3: wait
The ADNI Data Sharing and Publications Committee "generally reviews data use applications within two weeks". You get
the answer by email. After approval, **ADNI** appears in the **Select Study** menu when you log in to IDA.

## Step 4: keep the account alive

- Once a year IDA asks you to update your application. You get a reminder about a month before the deadline, and
  **the account expires** if you don't answer.
- During the update you can add or remove co-investigators and report publications.

## If you also want AIBL
AIBL (an Australian cohort with a protocol similar to ADNI) is also on IDA, with its own application and its own data
use agreement. The download steps in this guide are the same.
